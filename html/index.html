About TFRO

Regulating Tricycle Operations in Lucena City

The Tricycle Franchising and Regulatory Office (TFRO) of Lucena City is committed to the orderly, safe, and efficient regulation of tricycle operations across the city.

Through this Integrated Management Information System, we modernize the way franchise records, operators, drivers, and regulatory processes are managed — ensuring transparency, accuracy, and faster public service.

  • Centralized and secure franchise and rider records
  • Streamlined applications and payment processing
  • Reliable reports for data-driven governance
Proudly Serving Lucena City, Quezon
