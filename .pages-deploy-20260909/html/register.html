TFRO MIS registration agreement

Terms of Use and Data Privacy Notice

Effective September 8, 2026 · Version TFRO-TERMS-2026-09-08

Please read this notice before creating an account. It explains the rules for using TFRO MIS and how the Tricycle Franchising and Regulatory Office of Lucena City handles your personal data.

1. Purpose and acceptable use

TFRO MIS supports account verification, franchise and MTOP transactions, driver records, change-motor and renewal requests, violations, payments, reporting, and authorized public-service operations. You must provide accurate information, use only your own account, protect your password, and never misuse, alter, or access records without authority.

2. Personal data collected

The system may collect your name, email address, contact number, home address, franchise or enforcer ID, profile photo, vehicle and driver information, submitted documents, transaction and payment details, violations, and security or audit activity.

3. Why and how data is used

Your information is processed to verify identity and eligibility, provide TFRO services, review applications, maintain official records, communicate updates, protect the system, create required reports, and meet lawful government obligations. Only information reasonably necessary for these purposes should be collected and used.

4. Access, sharing, retention, and security

Authorized TFRO personnel and users receive access according to their role. Data may be handled by necessary technology providers, including database and hosting services, or disclosed when required by law. Records are retained only as needed for service, legal, regulatory, security, and audit requirements. TFRO applies access controls and other safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Your data privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to be informed, access or correct your data, object to certain processing, request erasure or blocking where allowed, receive data portability where applicable, lodge a complaint, and seek damages for unlawful processing. Contact the TFRO Office or its designated Data Protection Officer through the office's official contact channels to exercise these rights.

6. Consent and account approval

By selecting “I Agree,” you confirm that you understand this notice and accept the Terms of Use. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it by contacting TFRO; withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing and may limit services that require the affected data. Records required by law or official duties may still be retained.

7. Account action and changes

TFRO may restrict or suspend accounts used fraudulently, unlawfully, or against these terms. Material updates to this agreement should be presented for renewed acceptance. Questions may be directed to the TFRO Office through its official contact channels.
